## ArtMuse language

ArtMuse's application interface must be English-only. Use English for all
user-facing text, including menus, headings, subtitles, labels, buttons,
tooltips, notifications, validation messages, and generated report labels.
Preserve user-entered content and proper names in their original language.
Conversation replies may follow the user's language.
